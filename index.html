Why HomeBase exists

We built HomeBase because budgeting in our heads didn't work.

We were never great at spending. We could tell ourselves we were going to budget, be more careful, or stop making unnecessary purchases — but when the money was sitting in the account, it was easy to lose track of where it was actually going.

HomeBase started with a simple idea: seeing your money makes you more conscious of your money. When you can see what's coming in, what's going out, what's protected, and what you actually have left to spend, the decisions become real.

“It's a lot easier to change your spending when you can actually see the money flow.”

We also know how hard it is right now. Everyday costs are high, bills keep climbing, and it feels like money disappears faster than it used to. That's exactly why we believe now is the time to pay attention — not to make people feel guilty about spending, but to help people understand it.

HomeBase is being built to help ordinary households stay aware, stay organized, and make their money work harder for them.

We're building it for people like us.
